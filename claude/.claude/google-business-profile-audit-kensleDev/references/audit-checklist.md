# Audit Checklist

Use this checklist alongside the structured input to assess completeness and find issues.

---

## 1. Business information accuracy

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Business name matches real-world signage and trading name | Mismatch confuses customers and can trigger GBP suspension | Use the exact real-world business name, no extra keywords | Photo of shopfront/signage, website header |
| Address is correct and consistent across web | Inconsistent NAP (name, address, phone) hurts local visibility and trust | Ensure GBP, website, and directories match exactly | GBP listing, website footer/contact page |
| Phone number is correct and clickable on mobile | A wrong number means lost calls; a non-clickable number on mobile means frustrated customers | Use the primary business number, ensure `tel:` link on website | GBP listing, website contact page (mobile) |
| Website URL is correct and not a broken link | A dead link on GBP wastes every profile visit | Check the link works and points to the right page | Click the link on GBP |
| Opening hours are accurate, including special hours | Wrong hours lead to wasted journeys and bad reviews | Set normal hours and bank holiday/special hours in GBP | Shop window photo, website hours vs. GBP hours |

---

## 2. Google Business Profile completeness

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Primary category is accurate | The primary category is the strongest local ranking signal | Choose the single most accurate category from the GBP category list | Current GBP category vs. actual business activity |
| Secondary categories are used (where relevant) | Adds breadth without diluting the primary category | Add 2–4 genuinely relevant secondary categories | Current categories vs. services offered |
| Services/products/menu filled in | Helps customers understand what you offer before they call | List real services with brief descriptions; keep current | Current GBP services section |
| Description is present and useful | Tells your story in search results | Write 2–3 sentences about what you do, where, and what makes you different | Current GBP description |
| Attributes are set (wheelchair access, Wi-Fi, etc.) | Helps customers filter and find relevant businesses | Set every attribute that honestly applies | Current GBP attributes |
| Questions & Answers section populated | Answers common questions before they become phone calls | Seed 3–5 common questions with helpful answers | Current Q&A section |
| Posts or updates used recently | Shows the business is active; can promote offers or news | Post once a month minimum with a photo and a clear action | Recent posts tab |

---

## 3. Reviews and reputation

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| There is a simple way for customers to leave a review | If it is hard to review, only the angry will bother | Create a short review link or QR code; share after a good interaction | Current review link, current ask process |
| Reviews are being replied to | Replies show you care; Google notices engagement | Reply within a few days; thank positive reviewers; address concerns professionally | Recent reviews and replies |
| Negative reviews get a calm, constructive reply | A good reply to a bad review often impresses future customers more than the review itself | Acknowledge, apologise if appropriate, offer to put it right offline, keep it brief | Recent 1–3 star reviews and replies |
| Review volume is healthy for the area and industry | Customers trust businesses with recent, genuine reviews | Ask every genuine customer, not just the happy ones; use a review link or QR code | Review count vs. local competitors |
| No suspicious review patterns | Fake or incentivised reviews risk profile suspension | Remove any reviews you know are fake; never offer discounts for 5-star reviews | Review history — sudden spikes, copy-paste language |

---

## 4. Photos and trust signals

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Profile has a variety of photos (interior, exterior, team, work) | Customers want to see the real business before they commit | Upload 10+ recent, genuine photos; avoid stock images | Current photo gallery |
| At least one photo added in the last 3 months | An inactive profile looks abandoned | Add a new photo at least once a month | Photo upload dates |
| Cover photo and logo are set | First impressions count in local search results | Use a clear, well-lit photo for the cover; use the real logo | Current cover photo and logo |
| Photos are high-quality but not over-produced | Over-produced photos look fake; blurry photos look unprofessional | Use a modern phone camera in good light; avoid heavy filters | Current photo quality |

---

## 5. Website / mobile customer journey

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Site loads quickly on mobile | Every second of delay costs conversions | Optimise images; use a simple, fast host; test on 4G | Quick load test on mobile |
| Phone number is visible without scrolling | If a customer has to hunt, they will leave | Put the phone number at the top of the mobile homepage as a click-to-call link | Mobile homepage screenshot |
| Main customer action is obvious within 3 seconds | "Call now", "Book online", "Order here" — make it unmissable | Large, coloured button above the fold on mobile | Mobile homepage screenshot |
| Booking/order/contact form works end to end | A broken form is worse than no form | Test your own form monthly; ensure confirmation messages appear | Test submission + confirmation screenshot |
| Menu/services/pricing clearly displayed | Customers want to know what you do and roughly what it costs | List core services/products with descriptions; include price ranges if possible | Services/menu page screenshot |
| Site works on different phones and browsers | A site that only works on iPhone alienates Android users | Test on at least one iPhone and one Android device | Cross-device test |
| No broken links or pages | Broken links damage trust and waste customer time | Run a broken link check monthly | Link checker results |
| Contact information matches GBP exactly | Consistency builds trust with customers and search engines | Match GBP NAP on every page footer | Website footer vs. GBP |

---

## 6. Contact / enquiry flow

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Enquiry form sends a confirmation | No confirmation = customer wonders if you got it | Add an on-screen confirmation message and an auto-reply email | Test submission |
| Auto-reply or follow-up email is useful | A generic "thanks" is a wasted opportunity | Include what happens next, expected response time, and contact details | Received follow-up email |
| Missed call process exists | Missed calls are missed money | Voicemail is set up; calls are returned within a few hours; consider a text-back service | Test call outside hours |
| Response time to enquiries is reasonable | Customers expect a reply within a day at most | Set expectations on your contact page; reply within 24 hours | Current process description |

---

## 7. Local visibility basics

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Business appears for its own name search | If it does not, something is seriously wrong | Check for suspension, verification issues, or duplicate profiles | Search business name in local area |
| Business appears for relevant local searches | Shows whether your profile is properly configured | Search "[service] in [town]" and note position | Local search test |
| No duplicate profiles exist | Duplicates confuse customers and split reviews | Find and remove or merge duplicates | Search business name and address |
| Citations on major directories are consistent | Inconsistent information across the web weakens local presence | Check Yell, Yelp, Thomson Local, Facebook, and trade directories | Directory listings |

---

## 8. Risk / compliance flags

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| No keyword stuffing in business name | This is the most common GBP suspension reason | Use only the real business name. If it has keywords, remove them. | GBP business name field |
| No incentivised review offers visible | Discouraged by Google; can result in review removal | Remove any "discount for review" language from website and social media | Website, social media posts |
| No misleading or fake information | Can lead to profile suspension and customer complaints | Correct any inaccurate information immediately | Full profile review |
| Business is eligible for a GBP profile | Some online-only or virtual businesses are not eligible | Confirm you have a physical location or service area with face-to-face contact | GBP eligibility guidelines |

---

## 9. Quick wins

| Check | Why it matters | Good fix | Evidence needed |
|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Fix any wrong information immediately | Wrong info is the easiest fix with the biggest trust payoff | Correct name, address, phone, hours, website in one session | Current vs. correct info |
| Reply to the last 5 reviews | Quickest way to look active and engaged | Write honest, brief replies — thank happy customers, address concerns calmly | Recent unreplied reviews |
| Add 3 recent photos | Makes the profile look alive | Take photos this week of the shopfront, interior, or recent work | Current photo count |
| Set up a review short link / QR code | Makes it easy for customers to leave reviews | Create a short link from the GBP "Ask for reviews" section; print a QR code | Current review link |

## 10. Done-for-you opportunities

| Opportunity | When to suggest | Value to business |
|------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Profile cleanup session | Multiple errors or missing sections | Profile looks professional and complete |
| Review reply templates | Owner is not replying or replies inconsistently | Save time and maintain a consistent voice |
| Review request system setup | Low review count, no current process | Steady stream of genuine reviews |
| Website CTA/contact form fix | Broken or confusing mobile journey | More calls, bookings, or orders from the same traffic |
| Simple follow-up email/message flow | No post-enquiry follow-up | Convert more enquiries into customers |
| Landing page recommendation | Website is poor and a full rebuild is not needed yet | Quick win for a specific service or offer |
