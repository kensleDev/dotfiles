# Example: Green Lane Barbers, Leeds

This is a fictional report showing the intended style: practical, specific, clear, and concise enough that a busy barber will actually read it. The business is made up. The findings are realistic.

---

## Business reviewed

**Business:** Green Lane Barbers
**Type:** Barbershop
**Location:** Headingley, Leeds
**Website:** greenlanebarbers.co.uk (fictional)
**Date of audit:** June 2026

---

## Executive summary

Green Lane Barbers has built a strong local reputation — 86 reviews at 4.8 stars shows customers like what you do. The main problem is that your Google profile has gone quiet over the last six months and the website makes it harder to book than it needs to be. There are no risk flags. Four straightforward changes would make a real difference to how many bookings come through Google and the website.

---

## The 3 biggest opportunities

1. **Reviews have gone unanswered for months** — Replying to your last 10–15 reviews would show you are active and engaged.
2. **No online booking link on your Google profile or website** — Adding a booking link (even a simple one) would capture customers who do not want to call.
3. **Mobile website buries the phone number and has no booking button** — A customer on a phone has to scroll and search to book or call.

---

## What is working well

- Strong review base: 86 reviews at 4.8 stars gives you credibility that many barbers in Leeds do not have.
- The shop looks great in photos. The interior and exterior shots on your profile are genuine and welcoming.
- Your business name is clean — no keyword stuffing, just "Green Lane Barbers". This is correct and important.
- Your address, phone number and opening hours are accurate on Google.
- Your category ("Barber shop") is correct.

### 5. Quick wins

These are things you can fix in under an hour:

1. Reply to your last 10 Google reviews — 30 seconds each, done in 5 minutes.
2. Add a clickable phone number to the top of the mobile website — one line of code or a website setting.
3. Upload 3 new photos of the shop and recent cuts — take them on your phone today and add them.
