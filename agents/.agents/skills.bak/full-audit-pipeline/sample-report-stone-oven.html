Executive Summary

The Stone Oven Bakehouse has a genuinely loyal customer base and a strong star rating, which is a solid foundation. The two things holding it back are a Google Business Profile that is only partly filled in, and a website that is hard to read on a phone — where most of your potential customers are looking.

None of the fixes are complicated. The biggest wins come from completing the profile, adding a few recent photos, and making the mobile ordering journey simpler. This report sets out exactly what to do, in priority order.

The 3 Biggest Opportunities

1

Fill in the missing Google Business Profile fields

The profile is missing a service area, a proper description, and a menu link. Adding these takes 15 minutes and helps Google show you for more relevant searches.

2

Make the website usable on a phone

The site text is too small on mobile and the menu is a PDF that pinches to zoom. A readable mobile menu and larger text would stop people bouncing back to Google.

3

Ask happy customers for reviews more often

You get great reviews when they happen organically, but there is no systematic ask. A simple printed card by the till or a follow-up message would double the review rate.
